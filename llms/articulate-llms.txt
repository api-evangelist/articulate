# Articulate

> Articulate is an AI-powered platform for creating, collaborating on, and distributing workplace training at scale. Its Articulate 360 suite (Rise, Storyline, Review, Reach 360, Content Library 360, AI Assistant, and Localization) serves 125,000+ organizations across 187 countries and 133 million learners. Articulate does not publish a public developer REST API; its programmatic surface is the id.articulate.com identity provider (OpenID Connect / OAuth 2.0, Okta-hosted) used for SSO, plus standards-based e-learning content interop (SCORM / xAPI) produced by its authoring tools.

## Products
- [Articulate 360 Platform](https://www.articulate.com/360/): Creation, collaboration, distribution, and localization suite.
- [Rise](https://www.articulate.com/360/rise/): Web-based responsive course authoring.
- [Storyline](https://www.articulate.com/360/storyline/): Desktop authoring for custom interactive content.
- [Review 360](https://www.articulate.com/360/review/): Feedback and stakeholder review.
- [Reach 360](https://www.articulate.com/360/reach/): Integrated LMS for sharing and tracking training.
- [Content Library 360](https://www.articulate.com/360/content-library/): Pre-built training content and assets.
